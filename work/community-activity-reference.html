Two layers. Inside a world, Activity holds everything: every member's logs merged per person per day, plus messages, photos and notes. Members can like and comment, and only members can see any of it. The main Feed shows only deliberate posts: you tap "Share to Feed", and a post needs words and/or a photo, so a bare log can never be shared. Sharing creates a NEW post with its own thread, so comments made inside the world never leak out.
