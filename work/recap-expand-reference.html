A finished world never locks. On a recap chip, + adds one step instantly. Tapping the chip opens it in place into the full progress row: the bar, the next-point hint, +step / +2×step, ✎ to type any amount, and today's entries with ✕ to fix a mistake. Only one chip is open per tile, and the open one stays open after an add. Deleting the only entry reopens that check-in. Chips use two lines (name / amount), so names don't get cut off.
